Hi ~! I'm My (Lily), delighted to connect! 😊 I'm a quantitative researcher at Pi Associates in Ho Chi Minh City, and I'm deeply passionate about exploring research and industry opportunities in Finance, Data Science, and AI applications. I carry the humility of someone who has risen from small beginnings, and the eagerness of one who is now reaching for a long-awaited ambition.

My work focuses on systematic, data-driven approaches to financial markets, and sits between research and production. I build predictive models on noisy financial time series, and I care just as much about whether a model will hold up in the real world as about how well it fits historical data. That means a lot of time spent on validation, robustness testing, and finding out where models fail.

I'm also interested in how LLM agents can make the research process itself faster and more systematic.

**Research interests**

- Market microstructure and financial time series
- Regime switching and adaptive portfolio methods
- Model validation, robustness, and overfitting control
- Stochastic volatility and option pricing
- LLM agents for research automation

**Background**

I studied Applied Mathematics (Financial Engineering and Risk Management) at International University, VNU-HCM, on a full four-year scholarship. I've presented work on regime-switching contextual bandits for portfolio optimization (ACBES 2025) and on Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks for multi-horizon stock prediction (VSEFI 2025).

This blog is where I write up research notes, methods I find useful, and experiments that didn't work out.
